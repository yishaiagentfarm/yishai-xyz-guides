Website launch checklist
https://yishai.xyz/en/resources/website-launch-checklist/

[ ] Open the homepage and one service page on a phone
[ ] Follow the menu to a page and then to Contact
[ ] Submit an inquiry and receive it at the test destination
[ ] Complete the journey using a keyboard
[ ] Open old URLs and verify their destinations
[ ] Switch to the equivalent page in every offered language
[ ] Verify the site address and sitemap after launch

Record the page URL, device, expected result, and observed result for each check. When one fails, name the person fixing it and decide what to test again.
